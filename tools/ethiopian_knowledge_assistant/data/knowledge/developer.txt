The developer of this app is Samuel Abera Mekonn.

Samuel Abera Mekonn is a 3rd year Software Engineering student in Ethiopia.

He focuses on AI projects and is building AI technologies focused on the Amharic language.

He built ፍኖት Ethiopian AI HUB to make practical AI tools accessible for Ethiopian users, students, and developers.

His work combines language technology and productivity tools, including Amharic OCR, speech-related AI features, and a knowledge assistant powered by retrieval-augmented generation.

His goal is to develop reliable Ethiopian AI solutions for learning, creativity, and day-to-day problem solving.
