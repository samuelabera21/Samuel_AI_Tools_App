MetaAppz AI Hub is an Ethiopian AI tools platform built to make AI practical for everyday users, students, creators, and developers.

The platform focuses on Ethiopian and Amharic-centered use cases while still supporting general AI assistance. It combines language tools, utility tools, and educational tools in one place.

Core platform capabilities include:
- Amharic OCR for extracting text from images and scanned documents.
- Number conversion tools for Amharic and Geez formats.
- Ethiopian phone number validation.
- Ethiopian baby name generation.
- Ethiopic link shortening.
- Ethiopian and Gregorian date conversion.
- Amharic prompt-to-image generation.
- Free Amharic keyboard and writing support.
- Amharic text-to-speech.
- Random Amharic words generation.
- Ethiopian Knowledge AI Assistant for source-grounded Q&A.

The app includes a RAG-based knowledge system. For platform-specific questions, it retrieves relevant knowledge documents and combines them with model reasoning to provide accurate, grounded answers. For general questions, it can still respond as a normal AI assistant.

The overall goal of the platform is to make Ethiopian-language AI tools accessible, useful, and reliable across language, productivity, learning, and creative workflows.
